The method

Judge by expectancy, never by win rate.

The single discipline that runs through all six investigations: a 40‑percent‑win strategy with a 1:2 payout makes money; a 90‑percent‑win strategy paying +11 % loses it. High hit rate plus martingale is the classic account‑killer, and it is the shape most public “95 % consistency” bots take.

Hypothesis

What structural feature of the market would create an edge?

02

Sanity check

Does it survive the price generator — a cryptographic PRNG for synthetics, a near‑random‑walk feed for Gold?

03

Implement

Code it as a strategy against a clean event‑driven engine. No martingale, ever.

04

Backtest, net of cost

15k+ candles, every parameter variation, the real Deriv multiplier commission modelled in R. Under ~150 trades is inconclusive.

05

Verdict

Expectancy in R. Positive and robust → forward‑test on a demo account. Otherwise: rejected, on the record.

The six investigations

Every idea that was tested, and what killed it.

Each is reproducible from a script in the repo. Verdicts are marked as they landed.

01

Synthetic Volatility indices — M1 multiplier scalping

No edge

Hypothesis. M1 micro‑trends on V75 / V10 can be scalped with a break‑of‑structure + EMA + Stochastic setup at 1:2 risk‑reward.

03

Jump indices — the programmed jumps

No edge

Hypothesis. The jumps are large and “structural” — their timing or direction might be predictable, or the move right after one might be tradeable. 236 jumps analysed (jump = |log‑return| > 6σ local).

propertymeasuredimplication
inter‑jump intervalPoisson, gapCV ≈ 1.0memoryless — timing unpredictable
direction41–64 % up (n≈40–65)fair coin
post‑jump drift, 1–15 min≈ 0, sign flips by symbolnothing to trade

Deriv's own spec confirms it: Poisson arrival, 50/50 direction, CSPRNG. A straddle is negative‑EV once the two‑leg commission is subtracted.

No edge

With the measured frequencies and the real Deriv payouts, all 40 (symbol × contract × barrier) combinations have negative EV — best −1.7 %, worst −20 %. The house margin is 1.9–17 %; the real digit bias is ~0.3 % noise.

05

Gold / USD spot — the real‑market test

Gross edge < cost

Hypothesis. A real asset with trends, ranges and session effects should give technical analysis a fighting chance. 200,573 M1 candles of frxXAUUSD (Feb–Aug 2026, ~145 trading days). Four strategies, 23 backtests.

strategytradeswin %gross exp.net exp.
meanrev‑london16352.1+0.043 R−0.134 R
ny‑momentum22042.3+0.057 R−0.112 R
trend‑m1541640.6−0.047 R−0.098 R
session‑breakout50830.9−0.073 R−0.183 R

Intraday Gold on Deriv is near‑random‑walk (return autocorrelation ≈ 0 at M1/M5). Two strategies have a real but tiny gross edge — smaller than the multiplier commission, which becomes ~0.15–0.20 R on the short stops they need. Trend and breakout lose before cost.

Why

The limitation is the product, not the strategy.

Deriv's synthetic indices are a cryptographically secure pseudo‑random number generator. No pattern exists to find — not in the prices, not in the digits, not in the jumps. This is provable, and it was proven, five different ways.

Deriv's real‑asset intraday feed is near‑random‑walk on OHLC data, and the only contract fit for a stop‑and‑target strategy carries a commission that eats the sub‑0.1 R gross edges that do exist.

A genuine edge would need a market with real order flow and volume, and data beyond OHLC. A different broker. A different order of magnitude of capital and infrastructure.

What the repo is worth

A clean trading engine and a research loop that reaches an honest “no.”

Most public trading‑bot repos advertise win rates and hide the math. This one ships the tooling to measure expectancy honestly, and a written record of every rejected idea. The engineering stands on its own.

src/deriv/client.ts

Deriv Options API client — REST OTP handshake, WebSocket, req_id correlation, exponential‑backoff reconnect with stream re‑subscription.

src/risk/manager.ts

Account guardrails every order passes through: daily stop‑loss / take‑profit, hard floor, loss‑streak cooldown, concurrency caps.

src/learn/learner.ts

Adaptive layer: Thompson‑sampling bandit per bot × bet type, EV gate, kill‑switch with probation, parameter hill‑climb — state persisted.

ml/predictor.py

Online logistic regression (SGD), standard library only, talking to Node over a line‑delimited JSON stdio protocol. Degrades gracefully if absent.

tools/backtest.ts

Offline backtester for candle strategies with the real Deriv multiplier cost modelled in R. Gross vs net comparison built in.

tools/*-analyze.ts

Statistical analysis scripts — chi‑square, autocorrelation, variance ratio, transition matrices, jump detection, session characterisation.
